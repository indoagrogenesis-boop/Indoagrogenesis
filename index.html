IndoAgrogenesis is more than just an agricultural startup—it is a mission born from curiosity, passion, and a desire to serve farmers, nature, and the world. Created by three dedicated students from Bihar—Saurav Arya, Saurav, and Harsh—with a shared vision and a spirit of innovation, our journey began in the fertile heart of India where we saw the potential and deep need for truly effective soil restoration and crop enhancement. With inspiration drawn from our homeland and the struggles faced by countless farmers, IndoAgrogenesis was not founded by a single mind, but through the united efforts of Saurav Arya, who pioneered the core manure product, and Saurav and Harsh, who worked side by side to perfect the formula, build the brand, and extend our vision to reach and uplift communities everywhere. We believe agriculture is not only the backbone of our country but the foundation for a healthier, greener world, and so our products—manure, fertilizer, and more—embody the promise of regenerative growth and ecological harmony.

We stand together, equal partners in ingenuity and hope, determined to move from our humble beginnings as students to become leaders in sustainable scientific innovation. With each new idea and endeavor, we aim to benefit the nation, serve the environment, and leave a lasting impact for the entire planet. And as we prepare to launch our inaugural line of agricultural products, we ask for your attention, your support, and, where possible, your donations. Join us in a movement that believes every land restored, every crop improved, and every life enhanced brings humanity closer to balance with nature—something we deeply cherish as part of our roots and our future.
